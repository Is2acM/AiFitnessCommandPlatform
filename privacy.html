Privacy Policy

AIFit · Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AIFit accesses and handles information when you choose to connect your WHOOP account.

Information AIFit accesses

With your permission, AIFit uses WHOOP OAuth 2.0 to access your basic profile, body measurements, recovery data, physiological cycles, sleep activities, and workouts. AIFit does not receive your WHOOP password.

How information is used

The information is used only to display your personal health overview, answer your requests, and produce data exports for your own use.

Storage and processing

AIFit is designed to process WHOOP data locally on your device. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on your device in a file with restricted permissions. The current local application does not send your WHOOP health data or tokens to an AIFit-operated remote server.

Sharing and sale of information

AIFit does not sell your personal information. AIFit does not share your WHOOP data with third parties except when requested by you or required by applicable law. WHOOP remains an independent service and processes data under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored tokens remain on your device until you disconnect WHOOP or delete the local data directory. The AIFit application provides a disconnect action that revokes its WHOOP access and removes the local token. You may also revoke access through your WHOOP account settings.

Security

AIFit limits its local service to your device, uses OAuth authorization instead of your WHOOP password, and stores token files with restricted permissions. No method of storage can guarantee absolute security, so you should protect access to your device and account.

Your choices

You can decline or revoke authorization at any time. Declining or revoking access prevents AIFit from retrieving additional WHOOP data.
